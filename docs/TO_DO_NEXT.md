# To Do Next

## Priorities

### 1. Add item costing and profit reporting

- Record the cost of each menu item, based on its ingredients and current ingredient costs.
- Include those costs in sales reporting so each period shows revenue, cost of goods sold (COGS), and gross profit.
- Add operating expenses to the calculation so the report can also show net profit. Until expenses are recorded, label any net-profit figure as an estimate.

### 2. Send stock alert notifications

- Send push notifications when an item reaches its stock alert level to users who install the PWA and enable notifications.
- Verify delivery on supported devices and browsers, including permission granted or denied and the app running in the background.

### 3. Estimate weekly raw-material usage

- Estimate the quantity of each raw material used per week from average weekly menu-item sales and each item's recipe quantities.
- Show the date range and averages used so the estimate can be reviewed and updated when recipes change.

### 4. Track reorders and stock turnover

- Let users choose a date range and see how many times each item was reordered during that period.
- Use reorder frequency as a practical indicator of stock turnover, alongside quantities and dates where available.

### 5. Track items currently being ordered

- Record which items are included in an order that has been placed but not yet received.
- Show their order status so pending orders can be distinguished from available stock.

## Longer-term

### 6. Build an automated financial report

- Bring revenue, COGS, operating expenses (OPEX), and other relevant adjustments into one report.
- Calculate gross and net profit for a selected period, with clear breakdowns and traceable source figures.
- Treat this as a longer-term project that depends on reliable item costing and expense records.
