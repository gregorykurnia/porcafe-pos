Direction B · final

Canopy

The same warm, card-based workspace, under a lighter canopy: cream everywhere, forest green kept for the one thing that matters on each screen.

This is the reviewed and finished version of Canopy. It is the build reference for the Next.js 16 + Tailwind v4 rebuild. The rules behind every choice are in DESIGN_SYSTEM.md, and the reasoning is in CANOPY_REVIEW.md.

What changed from the first Canopy
  • ›Cream sidebar. The page reads as light, and green marks only what you can act on.
  • ›One solid green per screen. It is the focal action or figure; everything else stays quiet.
  • ›Five status tones, each with its own shape, so no status relies on colour alone. Honey is retired.
  • ›Cards hold sections; rows are ruled lines. Data screens get a compact density.
  • ›Daily close follows the paper sheet: one column in sheet order, with a sticky total and Complete on the phone.
  • ›Every text colour passes WCAG AA, figures are tabular everywhere, and touch targets are 44 px.

1 · System sheet

Tokens

Each colour has one job. Contrast ratios are computed live in this page from the hex values against the background named in the row: AA needs 4.5 for text, and 3.0 for large text and UI boundaries. Rows marked "decorative" are never used for text.

Type scale

Outfit for titles and big figures (its geometry echoes the wordmark). Figtree for everything you read and type. Tabular figures are on globally, so digits never shift as values change.

Spacing

A 4 px base. Gutters: 40 desktop · 24 tablet · 16 phone. Card padding: 24 (16 on phone). Gap between cards: 20 (12 on phone).

Radius and elevation

Icons

Outline only, at a 1.5 stroke, which is the closest match to the logo's line. lucide-react with strokeWidth={1.5}: 20 px in controls, 16 px inside chips and small buttons, 24 px in the phone tab bar. Never filled, never inside a decorative circle.

Motion

Motion only confirms something happened, so keep it short. With prefers-reduced-motion, everything becomes an instant fade except the Undo countdown bar, which turns into a number.

Line illustration

One small drawing, used only in empty states. It is a 1.25 stroke bowl and spoon, taken from the logo's line.

96 × 72 px · stroke ink-muted · fill on the bowl only, at brand-soft
2 · Component sheet

Parts, in every state

Each part is our own component built on a shadcn/Radix primitive, named in the corner of its card. States shown are the real ones from the content inventory.

3 · App shell

One structure, three widths

There are five sections. Sub-sections are always tabs at the top of the page, at every width, so the pattern never changes between devices. The sidebar shrinks to a labelled rail on the counter tablet, then becomes a docked tab bar on the phone. On every device, Daily close sits in the middle and is the easiest to reach.

5 · Counter tablet · 1024 px

The close at the counter

The tablet is first-class because it is touch-only and is where the nightly close is often typed. It uses the labelled rail, comfortable density throughout, 44 px targets, and the desktop two-column close.

6 · Screens · phone 390 px

One thumb from closing

The tab bar is docked to the bottom, not floating, so it never covers a row or a toast. On Daily close, a sticky totals bar sits above it with the running total and the Complete button.
