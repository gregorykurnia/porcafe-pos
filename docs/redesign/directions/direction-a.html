Direction A of three

Daybook

The app as a beautifully kept ledger. Pages, not dashboards: one calm column, figures set like type, sections separated by thin rules instead of boxes.

Navigation

Desktop: a quiet top bar with five sections as text tabs; sub-sections sit under the page title as a second row of tabs. Phone: a classic bottom tab bar with thin line icons.

Personality

Editorial, grown-up, unhurried. Large light serif numerals make Rupiah totals feel considered; everything else is plain, well-set Inter.

Space & radius

8 pt grid, but generous: 64 px between sections, 24 px inside them. Almost no containers — hairline rules carry the structure. Radius stays small and crisp (6 px on controls, 10 px on the rare raised sheet). No shadows inside the app.
