Mise.

Mise en place — everything in its place before service. A precise, tool-like workspace: a slim icon rail, split panes that keep the list and its detail side by side, and numbers set in mono so columns of Rupiah line up perfectly.

Navigation
Desktop: a 76 px icon rail for the five sections; sub-sections and the day/period live in a top toolbar, with ⌘K to jump anywhere. Phone: the section name is a button that opens a sheet of every section and sub-section; a contextual action dock sits at the bottom.
Personality
Calm, exact, quietly technical — like a well-labelled kitchen. Mono uppercase labels echo the wide-spaced wordmark. Colour is used only for state.

Space & radius

Strict 4 px grid; 48 px rows for touch, 40 px on desktop tables. Panes are separated by 1 px lines, not gaps — the screen reads as one instrument. Radius 8 px on controls, 12 px on panes and sheets. Shadows only on things that float (sheets, toasts, menus).
