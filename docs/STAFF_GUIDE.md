# Staff guide

How we use Porcafe POS on the phone and the counter tablet. Keep this to one page; when a screen changes, update the matching step.

## Before you start

- Open the app and add it to your home screen (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen). Push alerts only work from the home screen app on iPhone and iPad.
- Turn on alerts under **Setup → Notifications**. Stock shows "Alerts on this phone" at the top when they are on.
- The app needs a connection to save. If you are offline, the app shows what it has and refuses changes. Nothing is queued, so save again when you are back online.

## Nightly close (Daily close)

1. Open **Daily close**. It opens on today. Use the date control to pick another day.
2. Enter the portions sold for each item, then the payments (cash, card, QRIS).
   - Or tap **Scan sheet** and take a photo of the daily ticket sheet. See the tips below.
3. Read the review, then tap **Complete day**. If the review has something to check, the button says **Complete anyway**.
4. **Undo** is available for a short time after a day is completed.
5. Unfinished work? **Save draft** keeps it without completing the day.
6. On a day with no sales, tap **No sales** and confirm.

**Scanning tips**
- Lay the sheet flat in good light. Avoid glare and shadows.
- Keep the whole sheet and the date in the photo.
- In the review, compare each row (Saved against Sheet). Fix a row before you tap **Save day**.
- Saving a day that already has a record replaces it, and the button says **Replace day**.

## Receiving a delivery

1. Open **Stock → Orders**. Open orders are listed first.
2. Find the order and tap **Receive**. You can also reach it from the Stock tab with **Receive against this order**.
3. Enter what arrived and the received date, then save.
4. If something is short or damaged, write it in the notes.

## Weekly stock count

1. Open **Stock** and tap **Record stock count**.
2. Pick the material and enter what you counted, then save.
3. Do this for every material, once a week, at a quiet time.

## Ordering stock

- **Stock → To order** lists materials at or below their alert level.
- Tap **Create order** to start an order. The usual quantity is filled in, so change it only when you need to.

## Reports

- **Reports** has revenue, items, and weekday views.
- Selisih (the bank reconciliation adjustment) counts only once its month is over. Until then the month shows as open.

## Setup (for the manager)

- **Menu:** add items, set prices, and archive items you no longer sell. Archived items leave Daily close and scanning, and past sales stay in History.
- **Materials, Recipes, Suppliers, Import:** stock setup. Change these rarely, and check them after a menu change.
- **Notifications:** turn alerts on or off for this phone.
- **Data maintenance:** check legacy sales, duplicate dates, and preferred suppliers. Nothing here changes data unless you confirm a copy or a duplicate fix.
