# Staff guide

How we use Porcafe POS on the phone and the counter tablet. Keep this to one page; when a screen changes, update the matching step.

## Before you start

- Open the app and add it to your home screen (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen). Push alerts only work from the home screen app on iPhone and iPad.
- Turn on alerts in **Setup**, under **Notifications**. Stock shows "Alerts on this phone" at the top when they are on.
- The app needs a connection to save. If you are offline, the app shows what it has and refuses changes. Nothing is queued, so save again when you are back online.

## Finding your way

- **Computer:** a sidebar on the left lists the five sections: **Home**, **Daily close**, **Reports**, **Stock** and **Setup**.
- **Tablet:** the same five sections in a narrow rail on the left. The rail says **Close** for Daily close.
- **Phone:** the tab bar at the bottom lists **Home**, **Reports**, **Close**, **Stock** and **Setup**. **Close** is in the middle.
- **Sections within a page:** the tabs across the top of a page switch between views. Daily close has **Daily close** and **History**. Reports has **Revenue**, **Items** and **Weekday patterns**. Stock has **Stock**, **To order**, **Orders** and **Usage recap**.
- **Setup on a phone:** Setup opens to a list of its seven pages. Tap one to open it, and use the back arrow at the top to return to the list.

## Nightly close (Daily close)

1. Open **Daily close** (**Close** on the phone and tablet). It opens on today. Use the date control to pick another day.
2. Enter the portions sold for each item, then the payments (cash, card, QRIS).
   - Or tap **Scan sheet** and take a photo of the daily ticket sheet. See the tips below.
3. Read the review, then tap **Complete day**. If the review has something to check, the button says **Complete anyway**.
4. **Undo** is available for a short time after a day is completed.
5. Unfinished work? **Save draft** keeps it without completing the day.
6. On a day with no sales, tap **No sales** and confirm.

**Scanning tips**
- Lay the sheet flat in good light. Avoid glare and shadows.
- Keep the whole sheet and the date in the photo.
- In the review, compare each row (Saved against Sheet). Fix a row before you tap **Save day**.
- Saving a day that already has a record replaces it, and the button says **Replace day**.

## Receiving a delivery

1. Open **Stock**, then the **Orders** tab. It opens on open orders (**Open / On the Way**).
2. Find the order and tap **Receive**. You can also tap **Record movement** on the Stock tab. When the material has an order on the way, **Receive against this order** is offered there.
3. Enter what arrived and the received date, then save.
4. If something is short or damaged, write it in the notes.

## Weekly stock count

1. Open **Stock** and tap **Count stock**. Enter what you counted for each material. Blank rows are skipped. A difference over 20 % needs a reason.
2. To count a single material, use **Record count** in that row's ⋯ menu.
3. Do this for every material, once a week, at a quiet time.

## Ordering stock

- **Stock**, then the **To order** tab, lists materials at or below their alert level.
- Tap **Create order** to start an order. The usual quantity is filled in, so change it only when you need to.

## Reports

- Choose the period with the segmented control: **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 30 days** or **Custom range**. The button beside it shows the range in use.
- **Custom range** opens a popover with **From** and **To** dates. **Apply** uses the range. An end date before the start date is not accepted. While a custom range is in use, **Clear** in the same popover goes back to **This month**.
- The three tabs are **Revenue**, **Items** and **Weekday patterns**. The period applies to all three.
- Selisih (the bank reconciliation adjustment, entered on the Revenue tab) counts only once its month is over. Until then the month shows as open.

## Setup (for the manager)

Setup has seven pages: **Menu**, **Materials**, **Recipes**, **Suppliers**, **Import**, **Notifications** and **Data maintenance**.

- **Menu:** add items, set prices, and archive items you no longer sell. Archived items leave Daily close and scanning, and past sales stay in History.
- **Materials, Recipes, Suppliers, Import:** stock setup. Change these rarely, and check them after a menu change.
- **Notifications:** turn alerts on or off for this phone.
- **Data maintenance:** check legacy sales, duplicate dates, and preferred suppliers. Nothing here changes data unless you confirm a copy or a duplicate fix.

## Words we use

One meaning for each word, in the app and in this guide.

- **Daily close:** the screen where a day's portions and payments are recorded and completed.
- **Portions:** how many of each menu item sold on the day.
- **Payments:** the day's cash, card, and QRIS totals.
- **Soundbox:** the QRIS payment terminal (Nobu).
- **Sheet:** the paper daily ticket sheet you scan.
- **Selisih:** the bank reconciliation adjustment for a month. It counts once the month is over.
- **To order:** materials at or below their alert level.
- **On order:** supplier orders that have not been received yet.
- **Stock count:** what is on the shelf, recorded under Stock.
